Who we are

RELAY Dispatch is operated by [Business name] (ABN [number]) of [registered address]. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

Privacy questions, and requests to access or correct your information, can go to support@relaydispatch.com.au.

The short version

  • The free desktop app keeps your data on your own computer. We do not receive it.
  • Cloud plans store your data on our servers so your team can share it, which means we hold it on your behalf.
  • Your customers' information in RELAY is yours. We look after it, and we do not sell it or use it to market to them.

Information we collect

If you use the free desktop app

We do not collect your business data. The app stores jobs, customers, quotes and invoices in a local database on your computer. Our website and the app's own update check may record ordinary technical information such as your IP address and the version you are running, so that downloads and updates work.

If you use a Cloud plan

We collect the information needed to run your account and the service:

  • Account details — your name, business name, email address, mobile number, your role, and sign-in credentials.
  • Your business data — the jobs, customers, suppliers, subcontractors, quotes, invoices, timesheets, forms, photos and notes your team enters. This can include personal information about your customers.
  • Billing details — your plan, billing history and, for paid plans, the identifiers our payment provider gives us. Card numbers are handled by the payment provider and never reach our servers.
  • Usage and technical data — sign-in times, device and browser type, IP address, and records of errors, used to keep the service secure and working.

If you ask brny to do something

brny is our AI assistant. When you ask it a question, the instructions you type and the RELAY records it needs to answer are sent to our AI provider to generate the response. That content is not used to train third-party models, and brny only sees the workspace it was asked about.

Why we collect it

  • to create and secure your account, and to give your team access to your workspace;
  • to run the features you use, including syncing between devices, sending email and processing payments;
  • to bill you and to provide support;
  • to keep the service reliable, to fix faults and to prevent misuse;
  • to meet our legal obligations, including tax and record-keeping.

Cookies and analytics

The marketing site sets no advertising cookies and does not track you across other websites. The Cloud app uses the minimum browser storage it needs to keep you signed in.

If we add product analytics, we will list the provider here and keep it limited to aggregate usage rather than the contents of your records. [To be confirmed before publication.]

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information, and we do not give it to anyone to market to you or your customers. We share it only with the providers that make the service work, and only as much as each one needs:

  • Cloud hosting and database — stores the workspace data for Cloud plans.
  • Payments — processes card payments and subscriptions for paid plans.
  • Email delivery — sends quotes, invoices and notifications on your behalf.
  • Maps — resolves addresses for scheduling and routing.
  • AI provider — generates brny's responses from what you ask plus the records needed to answer.

The named providers for each of these, and where they store data, will be listed here in the approved version. [To be confirmed before publication.]

We may also disclose information where the law requires it, or to a buyer if the business is sold, in which case we will tell you first.

Where your data is stored

Cloud data is held in data centres operated by our hosting provider, currently in [region]. Some providers may process data outside Australia, including in the United States, under their standard contractual terms. [To be confirmed before publication.]

How long we keep it

Cloud data is kept while your account is active. After you cancel, we keep it for a short period so you can export or restore it, then delete it. Billing records are kept for as long as tax law requires. Backups are overwritten on their own cycle.

Keeping it secure

Cloud data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is limited to staff who need it to support you, and we keep audit records of access to workspaces. No online service can promise perfect security, so please use a strong, unique password and let us know straight away if you think your account has been accessed.

Your choices

You can:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct anything that is wrong;
  • ask us to delete your stored data, or delete it yourself from within the app;
  • export your workspace data at any time;
  • ask us to stop sending you product emails — we still send the account and billing messages that the service depends on.

Email support@relaydispatch.com.au and we will respond within 30 days. If you are a customer of a RELAY user rather than a RELAY user yourself, please contact that business first — we hold their data on their behalf.

Complaints

If you think we have mishandled your information, tell us and we will try to sort it out. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product changes. We will publish the new version here with a new “last updated” date and, if the change is significant, tell you by email or in the app.
